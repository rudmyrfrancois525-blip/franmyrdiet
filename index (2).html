Eat to perform.

The food and supplements I rely on to train hard, recover fast and stay lean.

About me

I'm Rudmyr François, an athlete who treats food as part of training. My plate stays simple: lean protein at every meal, clean carbs around my sessions, healthy fats for balance, and a short list of supplements that earn their place. Here is exactly what I eat and why.

Protein

Builds and repairs muscle.

Eggs

About 6 g protein each, plus choline and B12. My go-to breakfast.

Whey protein

Fast-digesting, roughly 20–25 g protein per scoop. Post-workout shake.

Chicken breast

Around 31 g protein per 100 g cooked, with very little fat.

Carbs

Fuel for training.

Rice

About 28 g carbs per 100 g cooked. Easy to digest and easy to portion.

Bananas

About 27 g carbs in a medium one, plus potassium. Quick pre-workout energy.

Healthy fats

Hormones, joints and steady energy.

Avocado

About 15 g fat per 100 g, mostly monounsaturated, plus fibre.

Supplements

Small additions to a solid diet.

Creatine

The most studied supplement for strength and power. 3–5 g daily.

BCAA

Leucine, isoleucine and valine. I sip them during long sessions.

Fish oil

Omega-3 (EPA and DHA) to support recovery, heart and joint health.

Magnesium glycinate

A gentle form of magnesium to support muscle recovery, relaxation and sleep.
